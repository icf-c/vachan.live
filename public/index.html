A text-to-speech reader for Chrome

Select text, or read the page. The sentence is marked and the spoken word is lit as the voice reaches it. Pause, go back a sentence, change speed, click a word to jump there, and save a page to a reading list. One player for every tab.

Three ways to get the voice.

Browser, the default

The extension uses the voices already in Chrome. No server, no account, no audio uploaded. Works for every language your browser can speak. Voice quality is whatever your system has.

Your own machine, for people who want a better voice

Install the open-source local server from the repo and point Vachan at it. That server runs Kokoro, so you get its 54 voices in 9 languages, on your machine. The page text goes to that server and nowhere else. No account.

Vachan cloud, the paid path

If you do not want to run Kokoro yourself, Vachan can synthesise on vachan.live. You sign in, and the extension sends an auth token and the text to be read. Audio comes back. One subscription, about $5 a month. Opens with the launch, early 2027. No account is required for the other two paths.

The extension has no analytics and no cookies. Browser voices and the local server never send text off your machine. The cloud voice sends the text you chose to read, and an auth token, to vachan.live to synthesise it. That text is used to make the audio. It is not sold, not used for ads, and not used to train a model. The only third-party script on this page is the bot check on the form. The form stores the email you submit and nothing else.
