Vachan

Reads the page aloud in your browser.
Open source.
Can be 100% local: nothing leaves your machine.

A text-to-speech reader for Chrome

Select text or read the whole page out loud, and as the voice reads the sentence is marked and the spoken word is lit the moment the voice reaches it so your eyes and your ears stay on the same line even when you look away and come back. 54 natural voices across 9 languages, from a neural model running on a server or your own computer.

  • Read aloud, read along. Sentence and word highlighting, a mini player on the page, a side panel with the queue.
  • Pick up anywhere. Pause, say that again, change speed, click a word to jump there, save pages to a reading list for later.
  • Your machine, your data. The voice is synthesised locally. No account, no upload, no tracking. No cookies, no analytics and no script from anyone, only the bot check on the form. Open source code from launch.
  • An open core cloud API. If you want vachan.live to do synthesis for you. One account, cheapest price per minute read and 100% privacy control, no tracking, so no one can sell your data.
